# Project brief: FlowChart
## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: Registering by December 18 and turning in my entry by December 23.
## The problem
- People in my school struggle with getting good study habits in or completing their work on time because they often get so many assignments they leave out one or two of them.
- Impact area: School and learning
- Why it matters (impact): They will be able to get ahead of their assignments and get study in everyday.
- Evidence it’s real: Many students turn in work late or do not finish it.

In a San Diego Unified School District survey, nearly 3 out of 4 students said they turned in work late or not at all at least once a month. Nearly 1 out of 5 said this happened more than once a week. Among students with late or incomplete work:
- What people told me: Person 1 tells me that he had to fit in homework, violin practice and swimming practice in his daily schedule usually forgetting and mostly pasteurizing his work. Person 2 told me that his grade was often lowered for not completing his homework.
## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by listing messy schueldling notes like do something at 10 or how long should I exercise but still have time to hang out with friends and do homework, which works better than a premade response as there can be different choices to choose from to be based on someone's opinion.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: MyStudyLife and Google Calander both stores class schedules, assignments, exams and send reminders in just one planner. But my website not only includes that but also a AI Helper that will help you with things you don't understand or need help on like how do I make this essay better, the AI will send a bunch of suggestions on making that essay better.
- Safety plan: Wrong or unrealistic schedule: The AI could misunderstand “practice at 10,” forget an assignment, or create a plan with too much work. I will make every answer show its assumptions and offer a way to correct them. Give several choices an tell the student to check the plan. Include a warning: “This is a suggestion, not a guarantee.” Test the AI with messy, incomplete notes before students use it.
## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. Schedule a student's schedule.
2. Help students with homework using AI
3. Messages from TeamSnap or something.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
